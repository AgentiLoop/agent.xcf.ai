FACT SHEET
App: AgentiLoop Agent! (shown as "Agent!" in the menu bar and in Finder)
What it is: AgentiLoop Agent! is the Mac app that puts an AI to work on the desktop: type what should happen, pick a provider, and the agent reads, writes, builds, clicks, and scripts until the job is finished.
Price: Free. Open source under the MIT License. Users bring their own API key for cloud providers or run local models at no cost. A PayPal donation is requested.
Platform: Mac with Apple Silicon; macOS 26.4 or later. English.
Distribution: Direct download from GitHub Releases as a DMG or ZIP, signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Not on the Mac App Store.
Public launch: April 12, 2026
Latest release: 1.1.9 (build 205), September 2, 2026
